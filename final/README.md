# Final — Integrated Pipeline and Results

This directory will bring together the leaf localizer, disease classifier, calibration, and explanations into the final pipeline.

- `predict.py` will take a photo and produce a result card with a leaf box, prediction, confidence, runner-up, heat map, and care note, or a retake or expert-consultation message.
- Store the final evaluation results and demonstration materials here as they become available.

Keep datasets out of the repository; use download scripts instead.
